Engine

My Setup

I ran 2 diffrent setups, although they are both Naturally aspirated my tuner RGHTBrainDesign and I noticed a 15% increase in airflow and the TPS read more accuratly in the Second Version.

Version 1:

The FC RX7 T2 and NA TPS only read accuratly read up to 30% until it spikes to 100%. This means if you press the gas pedal and the throttle opens, lets say for example 45% it will send the ECU a signaal as if it was 100% open. I would be a nightmare to tune and can make you loose performance. So its generally recommended to stitch out for an FD RX7 TPS (bolts into FC Throttle bodies) or the Mustang TPS for generic bodies like I used. That way the ECU accuratl reads TPS and do its work accordingly.

  • 13B from 1991 non-turbo RX7. Built by RJS Performance (built for 78psi of boost 1200HP crank or wheel I don't remember what he said)
  • S5 complete intake manifold (bored and powder coated black)
  • Generic Ebay 3" throttle body (back plate was welded on upper intake manifold inorder to bolt it on)
  • 1990 mustang v8 TPS with 3D printed adapter
  • Custom length throttle cable then I later switched out for FD cable.
  • GM style IAT
  • No idle control. That was a mistake esspecially using a generic TB.
  • IGN1A High Output Coils

    • Version 2:

    My main motive for changing to the FD intake was because the old NA S5 was super bulkey and alot heavier. It got annoying really fast having to remove the entire intake just to work on the exhaust or in that general areadue to the space constraint in the miata engine bay. The other reasons were because it looked better, eliminate the throttle body and tps issues I was chasing in the other set up, and due to the air flow increase. I didn't know it was 15% better until Ryan noticed it while tuning. I will further explain the fuel rail issues I had in the fuel section. But if I had to go back in time I would run the Turbo S5 lower intake manifold instead of the FD. Which would save cost on the extra gaskets and FC secondary fuel rail fit better than FD.

  • 13B from 1991 non-turbo RX7. Built by RJS Performance (can handle 78psi of boost 1200HP crank or wheel I don't remember what he said).
  • S7 FD RX7 Upper and Lower intake manifold
  • S6 FD Throttle body with Greedy elbow
  • OEM FD TPS, IAT, and Idle control solenoid
  • FD Throttle Cable
  • 13B S5 6 port to FD lower intake manifold adapter
  • OEM Metal Gaskets; FC lower intake to block, FD lower intake manifold, FD lower to upper intake manifold
  • IGN1A High Output Coils
