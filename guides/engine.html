Engine

My Setup

I ran 2 diffrent setups, although they are both Naturally aspirated my tuner RGHTBrainDesign and I noticed a 15% increase in airflow and the TPS read more accuratly in the Second Version.

Version 1:

  • 13B from 1991 non-turbo RX7
  • S5 complete intake manifold (bored and powder coated black)
  • Generic Ebay 3" throttle body (back plate was welded on upper intake manifold inorder to bolt it on)
  • 1990 mustang v8 TPS with 3D printed adapter. The FC RX7 T2 and NA TPS only read accuratly read up to 30% until it spikes to 100%. This means if you press the gas pedal and the throttle opens, lets say for example 45% it will send the ECU a signaal as if it was 100% open. I would be a nightmare to tune and can make you loose performance. So its generally recommended to stitch out for an FD RX7 TPS (bolts into FC Throttle bodies) or the Mustang TPS for generic bodies like I used. That way the ECU accuratl reads TPS and do its work accordingly.
  • Custom length throttle cable then I later switched out for FD cable.
  • GM style IAT
  • No idle control. That was a mistake esspecially using a generic TB.
