Engine

My Setup

I ran 2 diffrent setups, although they are both Naturally aspirated my tuner RGHTBrainDesign and I noticed a 15% increase in airflow and the TPS read more accuratly in the Second Version. On both versions I used the Lucky7Racing rotary subframe for the NA/NB.

Version 1:

The FC RX7 T2 and NA TPS only read accuratly read up to 30% until it spikes to 100%. This means if you press the gas pedal and the throttle opens, lets say for example 45% it will send the ECU a signaal as if it was 100% open. I would be a nightmare to tune and can make you loose performance. So its generally recommended to stitch out for an FD RX7 TPS (bolts into FC Throttle bodies) or the Mustang TPS for generic bodies like I used. That way the ECU accuratl reads TPS and do its work accordingly.

  • 13B from 1991 non-turbo RX7. Built by RJS Performance (built for 78psi of boost 1200HP crank or wheel I don't remember what he said)
  • S5 complete intake manifold (bored and powder coated black)
  • Generic Ebay 3" throttle body (back plate was welded on upper intake manifold inorder to bolt it on)
  • 1990 mustang v8 TPS with 3D printed adapter
  • Custom length throttle cable then I later switched out for FD cable.
  • GM style IAT
  • No idle control. That was a mistake esspecially using a generic TB.
  • IGN1A High Output Coils

    • Version 2:

    My main motive for changing to the FD intake was because the old NA S5 was super bulkey and alot heavier. It got annoying really fast having to remove the entire intake just to work on the exhaust or in that general areadue to the space constraint in the miata engine bay. The other reasons were because it looked better, eliminate the throttle body and tps issues I was chasing in the other set up, and due to the air flow increase. I didn't know it was 15% better until Ryan noticed it while tuning. I will further explain the fuel rail issues I had in the fuel section. But if I had to go back in time I would run the Turbo S5 lower intake manifold instead of the FD. Which would save cost on the extra gaskets and FC secondary fuel rail fit better than FD.

  • 13B from 1991 non-turbo RX7. Built by RJS Performance (can handle 78psi of boost 1200HP crank or wheel I don't remember what he said).
  • S7 FD RX7 Upper and Lower intake manifold
  • S6 FD Throttle body with Greedy elbow
  • OEM FD TPS, IAT, and Idle control solenoid
  • FD Throttle Cable
  • 13B S5 6 port to FD lower intake manifold adapter
  • OEM Metal Gaskets; FC lower intake to block, FD lower intake manifold, FD lower to upper intake manifold
  • IGN1A High Output Coils

    • Other possible options

    There is an entire list of Rotary engines you can use. They range from carburated to injection setup, from NA to turbo applications. I will go over other rotaries. Any other engine thats not the FC S4/S5 or the 13b RE Cosmo (not the 13b-REW from the FD) will need a custom engine mount since the Lucky7Racing subframe mounting points are based of engines with center iron mounting points. The RX8 13b Renesis is hated on for being weaker but it is the strongest naturally aspirated rotary made. There were two series made and on a side note only the Series 1 manual version (6port) uses 6 injectors instead of 4 like every other 2 rotor. The Series 1 Automatic (4 port) and Series 2 used 4 injectors. RX8 motors are currently the cheapest compared to any model rx7 motor and sadly its because it it alot harder to push more boost through them than an RX7's because ratios are set up for NA, thinner apex seals, and other reasons. I don't hate it but choose what you want for your set up. If you want an NA power house on a budget, the 8 is the way to go. Now onto the FD unlike the FC where the mounts are on the center housing they are on the rear housing. Built for boost from the factory it is one of the blocks that will cost you the most, weather from Mazda Japan or a used one. In the past couple of years the crate engine has more than doubled coming from Mazda. It used to be around $5,000 and now sits at a whopping $13,000 not including intake or any other accessory. If you find a 12a dig around a little more and you just might find dinosaur bones lol. Good luck with those and I hope you like carburators, thats all I have to say. Now onto the most priciest engine Mazda released in a car... The 20b. You problaby sold an organ (or someone else's) to get it and I don't blame you. If you need to rebuild it there goes another organ but well worth it. Sadly my build can't event relate since you'll need a custom subframe, diffrent harness, fitment, and more. Well the CD-009 I have could work with it but thats about it. You'd need to upgrade the fuel pump and a few other things. Now with tripple the rotors comes with tripple the head aches; Apex seals,E-shaft, oh and if that center iron gets messed up you just lost what made that engine and could take a while to source that part.
